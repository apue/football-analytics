# Academy report contract

The report is written for knowledgeable supporters, academy practitioners, and
families considering elite youth football. It should communicate both academy
value and career risk without requiring readers to learn internal tier codes.

## Editorial order

Use a top-down structure:

1. State the central claim: elite academies can create exceptional football
   value, while even their oldest youth squads offer no automatic professional
   career.
2. Show competitive height: how many players established themselves in the Big
   Five and other explicitly named high-level leagues.
3. Show professional breadth for families: establishment in any eligible
   professional league, higher-level professional leagues, and the Big Five.
4. Cross each level with durability: at least one qualifying season versus the
   configured sustained-season count.
5. Return aggregate categories to concrete player pathways.
6. Close with interpretation, denominator boundary, coverage, and causal limits.

## Visual contract

- Prefer exact horizontal bars or a level-by-durability matrix to area funnels.
- All nested level rows use the same denominator and establishment threshold.
- Keep one-season and multi-season results visually paired; do not place
  durability after league level as though it were another funnel stage.
- Show counts and percentages together.
- Do not expose roster-row counts, source-file counts, internal tier names, or
  pipeline terminology in the reader-facing narrative.

## Player table

Include each target player once. Show the final Juvenil A/U19 season and at most
two representative real league names from qualifying seasons. Prefer the higher
reporting band, then the league with more appearances. If multiple leagues
jointly form a qualifying same-level season, they may both be shown. Do not call
a one-off appearance “reaching” or “standing in” that league.

Use reader-facing evidence states such as `已确认` and `尚无法判断`. Missing adult
coverage is not a negative outcome.

## Claims and limitations

The denominator is conditional on reaching the named oldest youth squad; it is
not the probability that a child entering the academy becomes professional.
Results describe observed career conversion and do not estimate the academy's
causal contribution.

When adult-career coverage is incomplete:

- label observed positive shares as conservative lower bounds;
- name material missing league groups, such as professional second or third
  tiers;
- allow confirmed professional and high-level rows to coincide when the source
  covers only high-level leagues, and explain why;
- never infer that an unobserved player failed to become professional.

The report is local until the user separately confirms deployment.
